# Feature evidence matrix (film pre-production)

Audited 2026-11-11 against the repository at `d6c467f`, the local app (Next.js dev server, `NEXT_PUBLIC_VEIL_E2E_WALLET=1`, `EMAIL_TRANSPORT=dev-mailbox`), the testnet contract `veilprocure-106725.testnet` and the recorded journeys (`docs/TEST_EVIDENCE.md`, `docs/AI_VERIFICATION.md`). Every row names where the proof for a film claim comes from. Nothing below is assumed from the product specification.

Status key: **working** (exercised end to end and recorded), **partial** (works with a stated limitation), **missing** (not implemented, must not be shown or claimed).

## Capture technique

Chromium driven by Playwright at 1536 × 864 CSS px with device scale factor 1.25 → lossless 1920 × 1080 PNG frames through the DevTools screencast (frames are emitted on change, with timestamps; a constant 30 fps sequence is derived from them). A cursor is drawn in-page at the real pointer position because headless capture has no pointer. The Next.js development badge is hidden with injected CSS; it is not part of the product. No frame is edited; zooms and callouts are composited over the frames afterwards.
